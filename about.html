I’m a multidisciplinary artist, designer, and photographer.

As an artist, I make work to process the past, challenge pre-existing narratives, and create new meaning in the present. For me, art opens up the possibility for self-discovery, by bringing my inner and outer worlds into focus.

My work explores themes of subjectivity, nature, and home through writing, art, and storytelling. My writing, photographs, and illustrations have been published in The Globe & Mail, Incite Magazine, Occasional Dispatch, Aesthetic Magazine, and The Silhouette. My art has been exhibited at Analogue Gallery, SKETCH, and Humber College.

As a designer, my goal is to create clear, impactful, thoughtful, and accessible designs. I specialize in working with people and organizations who are committed to helping others. I am interested in editorial, UI/UX, branding, content strategy, and visual communication.

I have worked locally and internationally across several sectors such as media, healthcare, and government. With each project, I bring my skills, experience, collaborative approach, unique perspective, and care.

In 2012, I received my Honours Bachelor of Science degree in Life Sciences from McMaster University. In 2015, I graduated from Humber’s Graphic Design for Print and Web program. In 2019, I received a UI/UX Design Specialization from California Institute of the Arts and two certifications in Human-Centred Design from IDEO. I am completing my Master’s of Education in Counselling Psychology at the University of Toronto.
